# Traditional Indian Wedding Master Plan & Coordination Blueprint

## 1. Executive Summary & Event Itinerary
A traditional Indian wedding typically spans 3–4 days with distinct ceremonies:
- Day 1: Griha Shanti / Ganesh Puja (Morning) & Mehndi Ceremony (Afternoon/Evening)
- Day 2: Haldi Ceremony (Morning) & Sangeet / Cocktail Night (Evening)
- Day 3: The Wedding Day (Baraat, Milni, Varmala, Mandap Pheras, Vidaai)
- Day 3 (Late Night) or Day 4: Grand Reception

## 2. Standard Budget Allocation Guide
- Catering & Beverages: 30% - 35%
- Venue & Accommodations: 18% - 22%
- Decor & Production: 12% - 16%
- Attire, Jewelry & HMUA: 10% - 12%
- Photography & Cinematography: 7% - 9%
- Entertainment, Music & Choreography: 4% - 6%
- Favors, Gifts & Invitations: 3% - 5%
- Logistics, Priest & Contingency Buffer: 3% - 5%

## 3. Ceremony Details & Operational Logistics
- Mehndi: Bride henna starts 2-3 hours ahead; lounge seating, finger foods/chaat, guest mehndi artists.
- Haldi: Turmeric/sandalwood ritual; floor protection tarps, marigold decor, Phoolon ki Holi, quick-change rooms.
- Sangeet: Stage with AV, lighting, LED backdrop; family dance acts capped at 45-60 mins, DJ and buffet flow.
- Shaadi (Wedding):
  * Baraat: Band, dhol, permits, horse/car.
  * Milni: Gift garlands and matched relative greetings.
  * Varmala: High stage garland exchange.
  * Mandap: Vedic fire rituals (Saat Phere, Kanyadaan, Sindoor, Mangalsutra) lasting 45-75 mins.
  * Vidaai: Emotional departure, getaway car, luggage coordination.
- Reception: Red carpet/stage greetings, speeches, cake cutting, formal multi-cuisine banquet.

## 4. Milestone Planning Timeline (12 Months to Day-of)
- 9-12 Months: Budget, guest count, auspicious muhurat, venue & tier-1 vendor booking.
- 6-8 Months: Bridal/groom couture shopping, decor themes, DJ/choreographer hire, invitations.
- 3-5 Months: Menu tastings, priest samagri list, guest room blocks, jewelry finalization.
- 1-2 Months: Final fittings, shagun envelopes, baraat permits, welcome hampers.
- Week of Wedding: Master contact sheet, bridal emergency kit, shagun cash distribution.
